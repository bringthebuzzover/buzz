# Markets — commoditize & light-financialize Buzz campaigns

Brainstorm + parallel research (2026-08-11). **Not committed behavior. Not legal
advice.** Promoting anything here needs an explicit PRODUCT/UX decision and,
where relevant, securities/insurance counsel.

Related: [`ai.md`](ai.md) (matching, authenticity, content enrichment feed
**grades** and CPE bands that markets need).

## Thesis

Buzz’s asset is attributed campus attention (orgs × drops × linked posts ×
metrics). True Wall Street securitization of single drops is mostly theater.
What can work:

1. **Commoditize** graded spots / campus baskets / CPE indices.
2. **Sell physical forwards** (seasonal Campus Upfront) with take-or-pay.
3. **Add CPE collars** settled mostly in **inventory makegoods**, not cash.
4. **Fund ops** with brand prepaid credits / later RBF — not DIY “% of drop
   pool” notes to passive investors.

Cash-settled bets on engagement tend to look like **insurance, derivatives, or
securities**. Media history clears on **physical delivery + makegoods**.

## Practical stack (order)

```
commoditize spots / grades
  → Campus Upfront (physical, take-or-pay)
    → CPE collar (inventory makegoods + org holdback)
      → prepaid brand float / later RBF to Buzz Corp
        ✗ DIY revenue-share notes on a drop pool
        ✗ tokenized “campaign shares” as settlement
        ✗ open-ended cash CPE indemnity without a licensed partner
```

## 1. Outcome swap (soft) → Campus CPE Collar

**Verdict:** Pursue as a **capped SLA/collar with inventory makegoods + shared
org stake**. Park (or partner) cash-settled “Buzz pays the CPE delta.”

### Analogs that matter

| Analog | Lesson |
| ------ | ------ |
| IAB / 4A’s makegoods | Guarantees attach to controllable delivery (impressions); CPA/CPC-style outcomes usually *not* guaranteed — remedy is makegood flights/credits. |
| Programmatic Guaranteed | Seller absorbs under-delivery via reserved inventory; works when seller controls supply. |
| Hybrid influencer CPA | Base fee + upside; pure performance → adverse selection / gaming. |
| Affiliate networks | Survive via reserves, clawbacks, fraud gates — not faith in outcomes. |
| Amazon Vine | Sells process certainty (trusted supply), not star ratings. |
| Weather / parametric | Index settlement reduces disputes; basis risk is the product. |
| Hollywood slate financing | Whoever controls selection owns moral hazard — don’t guarantee brand-cherry-picked slates you can’t underwrite. |
| P2P insurance branding | Still licensed insurance underneath if cash payouts. |

### Buzz-native mechanics (sketch)

1. Brand buys a drop slate at a **quoted CPE band** for a defined settlement
   window (e.g. T+14 / T+30 after last linked post).
2. **Parametric index:** Buzz-linked IG metrics only (define likes+comments;
   optional saves/shares). No brand pixel as SOT.
3. **Collar:** below band → makegood (extra spots / next-drop credit);
   inside → done; above → no rebate; surplus feeds reserve / org bonus pool.
4. **Org holdback** (e.g. 10–25%) released on authenticity + min engagement;
   gaming forfeits into the pool.
5. **Buzz:** explicit guarantee premium into ring-fenced reserve; max cash
   liability = premium × k, then **inventory-only**.
6. Collar only on slates Buzz can underwrite / co-finalize.

### Disruptive variants

- **Brand mutual:** same-vertical brands pool semester makegood credits
  (Friendsurance-shaped; keep remedies as platform credits).
- **CPE put (not swap):** small premium for right to makegood if CPE > strike.
- **Inter-brand credit exchange + capacity lottery:** surplus credits tradeable
  among brands (expire fast); leftover org capacity raffled to collar buyers —
  stay credit-denominated (cash P2P variance ≈ derivative).

### Kill criteria

- Brands demand **cash refunds** as primary remedy at scale.
- Settlement depends on brand-owned analytics or disputed Meta gaps.
- Guarantee on brand-selected slates Buzz can’t underwrite.
- Org gaming (pods, spam, fake attribution) and holdbacks don’t bite.
- Correlated campus shocks blow the reserve repeatedly.
- “Guaranteed CPE” sold standalone to non-drop buyers (warranty-as-vocation →
  insurance).

### Open questions

- Empirical CPE variance by campus × org type × category × season.
- Will brands accept inventory credits as first remedy?
- Who finalizes collar slates (brand / Buzz / joint)?
- Org holdback elasticity before PLG apply rates die.

## 2. Revenue-share notes on a drop pool

**Verdict:** **Park DIY pooled notes.** Only-with-licensed-partner if outsider
capital must take a % of fees. Economic reality ≈ Howey investment contract;
labeled note ≈ Reves presumptive security.

### Analogs that matter

| Analog | Lesson |
| ------ | ------ |
| Hollywood slate | Pool ≠ safe; cherry-picking and opaque waterfalls kill capital. |
| Hipgnosis-style catalogs | Needs real audited cash + honest duration; don’t sell annuities on unseasoned fees. |
| Litigation finance | “% if success” works inside regulated fund wrappers, not SPA IOUs. |
| RBF (Clearco / Pipe / Capchase) | Advance vs *existing* trackable revenue to a **cap** — Buzz as borrower, not retail pool notes. |
| Spotter-style creator advances | Needs trailing catalog cash; Buzz drops are forward production. |
| WBS (Dunkin’, Domino’s) | Right architecture (SPV, cash traps); wrong scale for early Buzz. |
| Ag production / cash-forward | Best non-security template: commercial buyer of goods for delivery. |

### Prefer instead (same economic intent)

1. **Prepaid brand credits** (use-or-lose / rollover) — customer float, not yield.
2. **MSA + volume rebate** to that brand only.
3. **Later:** factor signed brand SOWs / RBF loan to Buzz Corp.
4. **Weird:** category syndicate SPV (brands co-fund shared inventory warehouse;
   Buzz allocates) — antitrust review.
5. **Weird:** parametric inventory “drop bond” settling in **SKU/fulfillment
   credits** to commercial counterparties + org co-op junior in-kind claim —
   cash yield investors stay out.

### If licensed pool someday

- Asset = Buzz brand fees on hard **Cleared** drops only (SOW + shipped + ≥1
  linked post + no early chargeback). Engagement = bonus tranche, not principal.
- Waterfall: ops/COGS reserve → senior inventory lenders → mezz capital →
  Buzz first-loss residual.
- Diversification covenants; seasoning; no advances against “pipeline.”
- **Scale guess (speculation):** revisit only with roughly $5–10M annualized
  cleared fees, 50–100+ independent cleared drops/year, boring concentration —
  not before.

### Kill criteria

- Cleared definition needs discretionary Buzz judgment investors must trust.
- Capital marketed for yield to non-users of drops/inventory.
- Pool funds general runway/R&D (Reves motivation → security).
- Top brands dominate the pool; fulfillment losses eat fees.
- Campus optics: extracting cash yield from student social capital for hedge
  funds.

## 3. Campus inventory forward → Campus Upfront

**Verdict:** **Sequence after commoditization.** Seasonal physical forward with
hybrid makegoods once the spot unit is repeatable; park cash CPE futures /
exchange UI until then.

### Analogs that matter

| Analog | Lesson |
| ------ | ------ |
| TV / streaming upfront | Seasonal commit for scarcity; continuous books struggle. |
| PG vs soft PMP | Soft holds let buyers not spend — v1 needs real take-or-pay. |
| Meta / Google reservation | Platform owns the short; hold windows + cancel penalties. |
| Ag forwards + basis | Standardize grade/location; named delivery ≠ exchange grade. |
| Renewable PPAs / VPPAs | Cash CFDs inherit nasty basis risk — prefer physical + makegood. |
| AWS RI → Savings Plans | Rigid SKU commits fail; flexible basket commits win. |
| University pouring rights | Brands already buy multi-year campus access; sell category *windows*, not forever monopolies. |
| StubHub “ghost” inventory | Never short spots you can’t source. |

### Buzz-native contract (sketch)

- **Unit:** `1 Campus Spot-Forward` = one accepted org seat in a drop on a
  defined **campus basket × academic season × grade** (not named-org-only).
- **Grades:** A/B/C by campus tier, verification, prior fulfillment
  (authenticity/matching scores optional inputs — see [`ai.md`](ai.md)).
- **Tenor:** Move-in / Rush / Homecoming / Spring / Grad — crop calendar, not
  fiscal Q3 alone.
- **Settlement:** physical default; hybrid makegood (substitute grade/campus,
  extra next season, partial cash last); cash CPE CFD only after a boring index.
- **Who is short:** Buzz capped vs history → org calendar pre-commits →
  multi-brand netting + assignment.
- **Defaults:** brand take-or-pay / deposit; Buzz published makegood ladder;
  org no-show → reliability hit + substitute.

### Bootstrap path

1. Commoditize spot language + indicative CPE bands (watermarked non-settlement).
2. Bilateral RFQ (few brands, one season, baskets only).
3. **Campus Upfront week** — seasonal auction, take-or-pay PG.
4. Posted Mid/Ask curves after ~2 seasons.
5. Index + basis; secondary assignment with Buzz consent.
6. Only then continuous book / cash CFD.

### Disruptive variants

- **Deliverable-grade campus futures:** `BIG10-FALL-GRADE-A × 10 spots` with
  published substitution list (CBOT-style grades).
- **Org calendar put / “rush insurance”:** orgs pre-commit capacity for a floor;
  brands buy guaranteed supply — solves naked short.
- **Weird:** contingent spots that activate on rivalry weekend / enrollment
  triggers (check event-contract / gambling optics).

### Kill criteria

- Brands refuse deposits / take-or-pay.
- Fill rates can’t support shorts without high walk rate.
- Attribution/CPE disputes dominate settlements.
- No calendar premium (demand only for this week’s drops).
- Secondary becomes speculative ghost inventory.

## 4. Tokenized “campaign shares”

**Verdict:** **Avoid** as settlement or fundraising asset.

Same optionality without the grenade: transferable assignment of locked spots,
Savings-Plan baskets, options on forwards, prepaid credits — off-chain contracts
with Buzz as registrar. Tokens attract the wrong liquidity (traders ≠ campus
brands), muddy securities/commodities lines, and student-org optics are toxic.

---

## Cross-cutting principles

1. **Remedy in inventory/credits first**; cash last and capped.
2. **Parametric on Buzz’s own attribution** — reduce measurement fights.
3. **Skin in the game for orgs** (holdbacks / reliability grades).
4. **Never let the adverse selector also dump risk** onto Buzz without
   underwriting (slate lesson).
5. **Never short ghost inventory** (StubHub lesson).
6. **Customer float before investor yield** (prepaid > notes).
7. **Academic calendar is the underlier** more than IG vanity metrics.
